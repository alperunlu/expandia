# Expandia

Expandia is a fast, turn-based territory conquest game. Every match is a race against the clock — pick from four resource types, each strong against one and weak against another, and claim the grid one cell at a time before your opponent does.

## Gameplay

- **Resource chain:** Military → Culture → Science → Economy → Military. Attacking with the type that beats your target's type gives a power bonus; attacking into the wrong type costs you.
- **Capturing:** Tap an empty cell adjacent to your territory to claim it instantly, or attack an enemy cell to battle for it. Captured cells always start at level 1.
- **Fortify:** Tap your own cell to raise its level (up to 9) and change its type.
- **Streaks:** Consecutive wins build a power bonus (+1 at 3 wins, +2 at 5); a single loss resets it.
- **Overdrive:** Winning battles charges an Overdrive meter (its size scales with map size). Once full, it instantly seizes every cell directly bordering your territory — empty or enemy. Both the player and the AI can use it.
- **Win condition:** Control the entire grid.
- **Maps:** Quick (5×5, ~2 min), Classic (8×8, ~5 min), Epic (10×10, ~9 min).
- **Difficulty:** Easy, Normal, Hard — affects AI aggression, defense, and Overdrive usage, plus a battle-chance bonus for the player on Easy.
