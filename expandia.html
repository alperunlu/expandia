EXPANDIA

Privacy Policy — Last updated: August 2026

Expandia is a single-player strategy game. This policy explains what data the app collects — which is nothing.

No Data Collection

Expandia does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or registration
  • Does not connect to the internet or any remote server during gameplay
  • Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • Does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos
  • Does not contain in-app purchases
  • Stores no data outside your device

Local Storage Only

Any game state (such as your current match) is kept only in the app's memory on your device while you play, and is not saved, backed up, or synced anywhere. Closing the app clears it.

Children's Privacy

Because Expandia collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an online feature — this page will be updated and the date above will reflect the change.
